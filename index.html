The real problem

Boutique agencies are getting squeezed from both sides.

Marketplaces solved speed for large shops. For boutique agencies they created a new trap: you cannot compete on response time AND you cannot afford the back-office to scale. Autopilot is the answer.

01

You cannot match marketplace speed on your own

Practices want same-day fills by 7 AM. A marketplace texts 200 hygienists in seconds. You are still scrolling contacts. The relationship advantage evaporates when the chair is already filled before you call.

02

Back-office eats your margin before you scale

Credentialing, invoicing, payroll, and reporting each need someone watching them. Hiring that ops team costs more than the placements justify. You are stuck in a size where hustle does not scale.

03

Marketplaces clip your margin AND take your hygienists

Every platform placement pays them 10 to 25 percent of your gross billing. Then the marketplace texts your hygienist for the next shift, building loyalty to the app instead of to you. You lose twice.
