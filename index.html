The market problem

Marketplaces took speed.
They took margin too.

TempMee, GoTu, and Cloud Dentistry solved the volume problem for large agencies. For boutique shops, they introduced three new ones.

01

They clip 10 to 25 percent of every shift

Every placement you make through a marketplace is a commission you're paying for a shift your relationship closed. That cut compounds across your whole book. At scale it is not a fee -- it is a partner you never agreed to.

02

They own the hygienist, not you

When a hygienist signs into a marketplace app, your relationship becomes a data point in their roster. Their loyalty is to the platform that texts them first. Your decade of trust becomes a commodity.

03

Speed still beats you even when you're better

Practices want same-day. They do not care how good your relationships are if the marketplace fills the chair at 7 AM before you finish your morning coffee. Boutique agencies are losing on response time alone.

What Brystle does

Your ops stack.
Nothing you don't need.

Four capabilities that cover the full agency workflow, from shift request to verified placement.

Same-day SMS dispatch

Open a shift. Brystle sends an SMS blast to your qualified roster ranked by fit, distance, and history. Hygienists reply to claim. You confirm. The practice gets a fill notification. No app required for hygienists.

Core feature

Full hygienist credentialing

License verification, immunization records, CPR cert, malpractice COI, OIG sanctions check, and OSHA bloodborne pathogen training -- tracked and auto-flagged before expiry. Every hygienist on your roster stays current.

Compliance

Multi-agency from day one

Each agency you run keeps its own brand, roster, and practice relationships. A holding company or staffing group can operate multiple brands on a single Brystle account. No bleed between books.

Multi-tenant

Practice portal plus hygienist PWA

Practices submit shift requests through a branded portal tied to your agency. Hygienists get a lightweight progressive web app for schedule, upcoming shifts, and credential status. Your logo. Your relationship.
